HOW TO INSTALL GLASSWAVE ON A MAC
=================================

Glasswave for Mac is free. It's in beta, and for now it's made for Macs with
Apple chips (M1, M2, M3, M4 and later). It doesn't run on older Intel Macs yet.
Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu > About This Mac: "Chip: Apple M..."
means it will work.


1. INSTALL
----------
1. Open Glasswave-Mac.dmg (in your Downloads).
2. Drag Glasswave onto the Applications folder in the window that opens.
3. Close that window. You can eject the "Glasswave" disk in Finder's sidebar.


2. OPEN IT THE FIRST TIME
-------------------------
Glasswave isn't signed by Apple yet, so the first time your Mac stops it:

1. Open Applications and double-click Glasswave.
   Your Mac says Apple "could not verify" Glasswave. Click Done.
2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
3. Scroll down to Security. Next to "Glasswave was blocked...", click Open Anyway.
4. Type your Mac password (or use Touch ID) if it asks, then click Open Anyway again.

That's only needed once. After that, Glasswave opens like any other app.

No "Open Anyway" button?
Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities > Terminal), paste this line and press Return:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Glasswave.app

Then open Glasswave again.

(On older macOS, before macOS 15 Sequoia, you can also right-click Glasswave
and choose Open.)


3. UPDATES
----------
Glasswave keeps itself up to date. When a new version is out, it downloads it
in the background and the Update button says "Restart to update". Click it, or
just quit Glasswave (Cmd+Q): the new version is put in place for next time. Your songs,
playlists and settings stay.

If your copy is from before November 16, 2026, download Glasswave once more from
the website to get automatic updates.

New things come to the Mac at the same time as Windows. Features that are only
on the phone app come to the Mac once they're added to the computer version.


4. NOT ON THE MAC YET
---------------------
- Macs with Intel chips
- An icon in the menu bar at the top of the screen
- The keyboard's play/pause and skip keys, and shortcuts from other apps
- Showing what you're playing on Discord
