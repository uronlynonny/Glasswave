Questions

Good to know.

Is Glasswave free?

Yes. Glasswave is free, with no ads and no account needed to start listening.

Can Glasswave play my Spotify playlists?

Yes. Sign in to Spotify or paste a playlist link, and Glasswave plays each song from YouTube Music.

Which phones does Glasswave work on?

Any Android phone or tablet on Android 8.0 or newer. The download is about 3 MB.

Who made Glasswave?

Glasswave is made by nonny dev.
